How these work. A checkpoint reads a conversation and writes down where it stands: what's on the table, who said what, where people disagree without having noticed, what the rules say, and what to look at next. It reports the room — it never decides for it.

One file per room. Re-running /checkpoint overwrites the page rather than adding one, so history lives in git and this index stays short. Each page states its own age and flags itself once it's more than seven days old.

Team rooms only. Checkpoints on 1:1 or private channels are delivered as a direct attachment and are never written here.
